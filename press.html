About:

Oh hello there, dearie..

You are a little old lady who needs to clean your big magical cellar. Learn colorful spells, meet strange NPCs, and uncover the secrets of the dungeon.

You die 100s of times and lose your memories in order to learn dark magic. it's cute!

Features:

  • Learn Glitch Magic that changes the rules of the game!
  • Tidy up!
  • Sweep away dust, monsters, bugs and more with your trusty broom.
  • Every creature has a unique mechanic!
  • Use the environment to solve puzzles and find secrets.
  • Free the elders trapped in the crystals.
  • Purify the dungeon, purify the world.
  • Be a Babushka!!!
  • Your cat ran away! (she's a babushka too)
